# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation welcoming regardless of experience, identity,
background, or disability. Be respectful, criticize ideas and evidence rather than
people, make learning accessible, and acknowledge uncertainty.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention,
threats, doxxing, plagiarism, knowingly fabricated results, and sustained disruption.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact
method listed in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers should minimize disclosure, avoid conflicts
of interest, and respond proportionally and consistently.

This compact policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1. Where more process is
needed, maintainers should apply that covenant's enforcement principles.
